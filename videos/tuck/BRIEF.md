## Intent
The user requests a completely new software/AI launch film with a subtle human opening, short walkthrough, human callback ending, minimal people and no interviews. Tuck is a fictional AI expense-preparation product. Physical receipts become an exact digital expense report. This concept was selected internally under the existing autonomous production instruction.

Considered five concepts: receipt-to-report (selected for physical/digital continuity); research tabs-to-answer; transcript-to-video edit; project updates-to-client report; screenshot-to-bug ticket. The receipt concept provides a fresh physical hook and a simple, visibly checkable result.

## Customizations
30-second landscape film, warm charcoal and amber direction, cinematic overhead hand/receipt footage, accurate authored UI, new local narration and score. Two primary interactions: extract receipt details, create report. Example amounts total $178.40; no tax or reimbursement claims. People are secondary: roughly 4 seconds at the opening, 3 seconds near the end.

## Execution
User authorizes concept → generate → assemble → review → improve → finished MP4. Continue through production and render without checkpoint questions. One new run ledger at artifacts/library-loop-12/budget.json, hard cap $10 estimated generation. Prior model research and price records inform choices; verify pricing only before spend. No previous generated scenes are reused. Narration, script, UI and edit are new.

Design decision: blue-professional remixed to warm paper #F5F0E8, charcoal #171A18 and dark amber #965B16; keep Space Grotesk and Inter.
