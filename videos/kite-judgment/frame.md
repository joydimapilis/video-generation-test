# Brand direction
Dark editorial kinetic typography, generous negative space, physical paper-like objects. Onest 550-750; headlines 100-130px, object labels 34-44px, support 30-38px. Crisp lines, 2px outlines, very shallow offset extrusions, no glow, no gradient, no AI iconography. Orange is a route and decision-boundary cue, not a success status. Warm near-white sheets are a tonal extension of white. One continuous visual system; wordmark held until final four-second lockup. Never distort, trace or recolor official logo; use white PNG at natural ratio. No UI components. No decorative counters or chapter numbers. Meaningful labelled work objects Research, Message, Page, Walkthrough, Email, Plan, Drafts. Objects align with short spatial moves, no excessive zoom. Opening deliberately frictional, latter half calm. Typography can occupy full video frame because there are no subtitles or VO. Closing includes Add Kite to Slack and kite.ai, no unverified badges.
