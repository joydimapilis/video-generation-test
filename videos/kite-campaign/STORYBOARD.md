## Video direction

Brand-remixed Blue Professional: white, ink #1D1C1D, blue #347BCD; local Onest. Exact illustrative Slack interaction. Client Relay uses its own expressly authored campaign palette cream #F4F1E8, navy #182B32, lime #D4F578 inside campaign surfaces only. No gradients, shadows, invented numbers or social proof. Readable large text, clear geometry, single moving subject at a time. Sound: original 120BPM electronic score with gentle percussive marks at semantic changes. No human scenes, stock, or paid generative media. Apply installed text-stagger rise recipe without shimmer/blur. Use dynamic-content-sequencing and spring-pop-entrance restrained smooth register. Clean cuts between frames; no cross-frame moving-object handoffs promised. The last two seconds of each major reading state are deliberate holds. Nothing important below y890. Every UI shot says “Illustrative campaign”. Reference gate passed before planning. All scenes built serially under inline worker fallback; no child process delegation required.
