Required workflow for people

Do not generate the main people-focused shots directly from text-to-video.

Instead:

1. Generate a high-quality still image of the person first.
2. Review the image for realism and consistency.
3. Only use images that look convincingly realistic.
4. Use the approved image as the reference/start frame for image-to-video generation.
5. Animate it with controlled, natural movement.

The goal is to improve:

* Human realism
* Face quality
* Identity consistency
* Skin texture
* Hands and body proportions
* Clothing consistency
* Scene-to-scene consistency

If the same person appears in multiple shots, reuse the same character reference or closely related approved reference images so their identity stays consistent.

Image quality

Before generating the video clip, make sure the source image has:

* Natural facial proportions
* Realistic skin texture and imperfections
* Natural hair
* Realistic hands when visible
* Realistic clothing textures
* Natural lighting
* Appropriate depth of field
* No overly polished or plastic-looking skin
* No obvious AI artifacts
* No distorted fingers or facial features

If the image does not look realistic enough, regenerate the image before moving to video.

Do not animate a weak source image.

Motion prompting

When converting the image to video, prefer subtle and realistic movement.

Use:

* Natural blinking
* Small eye movements
* Subtle facial expressions
* Small head movements
* Natural breathing
* Realistic posture shifts
* Controlled hand movement
* Natural hair and clothing movement

Avoid unnecessary large gestures or exaggerated movement unless the scene genuinely requires it.

Product and story

Keep the video focused on a product, software, technology, AI tool, or digital service.

The product should remain clear throughout the video.

Make sure the viewer understands:

* What the product is
* What problem it solves
* How the person uses it
* What result or benefit they get

Do not make the product too subtle or leave the viewer confused about the context.

Improvement loop

concept → generate character images → approve internally → image-to-video → assemble → evaluate → improve weak scenes → regenerate → final review

1. Check the source image.
2. Improve or regenerate the image first.
3. Adjust the image-to-video prompt.
4. Change the video model if necessary.
5. Regenerate the shot.

Do not accept poor human realism just because the generation technically succeeded.
