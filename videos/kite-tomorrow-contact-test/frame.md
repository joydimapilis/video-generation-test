# Comparison frame

A neutral review surface: current motion, new still and new motion simultaneously at equal scale. Canvas 1920×560; charcoal #171717, white #FFFFFF and gray #BEBEBE. Local Onest at 30px for headings and 23px for annotation. Three 640×360 images aligned at y=100, static labels at x=24/664/1304. Footer states native speed and the shared settings. No decorative animation, color treatment or sound. The central static image is explicitly identified as a still. Original scene design is preserved in the media.
