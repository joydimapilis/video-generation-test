## Video direction

This film tells product marketers that Kite can turn a brief into a draft, then respond to feedback. A fictional Relay release keeps the story concrete without inventing Kite features. The product surfaces are labelled “Illustrative workflow”; they are not a recording of a logged-in account. Public Kite documentation supports drafts, Preview/Review controls and thread-based revisions.

Use the brand-remixed Broadside system: dark #1D1C1D, white, and blue #347BCD. Onest display/body. No decorative grids, glows, or dashboard filler. Composition mechanics follow installed skills. Larger type and precise component motion; no scaling live text nonuniformly. 10s per frame, clean intentional cuts at 10s and 20s; no continuous-object seam promise. The first photographic beat is a metaphor for getting notes moving, not footage of a Kite feature. One instrumental track, no speech, no captions required. Review video plate before acceptance. General reference review gate passed before this plan; Phase 2 excluded.
