# Crumb art direction
Warm neighborhood bakery register, like a printed paper order ticket. Palette: flour #F5EBDD, espresso #30221D, brick #943D2B, pale oat #E5D4BD. Display serif and legible bundled sans (local CrewSerif and CrewSans font files). Deliberately flat paper backgrounds with dark ruled lines, large editorial headlines, physical pastry as the focal point; no artificial face treatment. Display 96–128px, UI 36–56px, captions 44px, labels at least 24px. Brand wordmark top left; service description top right; small fictional-concept disclosure bottom. Warm live footage cut into matching cream UI. Focus follows one interaction. GSAP easing-and-stagger adapter with 0.06-second stagger and 0.45-second settle; restrained press-release-spring for one button. No busy ambient decoration over people.
