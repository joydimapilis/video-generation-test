# ORRIN: Built to see in the dark

One original 30-second 16:9 dark industrial hardware launch film, delivered as
H.264/AAC MP4. The user delegates every technical decision and reviews only the
final video. This run has its own hard $10 estimated cap and its own ledger at
`artifacts/library-loop-2/budget.json`.

ORRIN is a fictional precision optical sensor module. It is not a real product,
company, or capability claim. A footer identifies the piece as a concept film.

## Direction
Deliberately the inverse of the previous Cue film: dark instead of light, no
dialogue instead of a spoken hook, materials and machining instead of interface
walkthrough. Derived from a second library slice of eight hardware, field and
milestone launches, chiefly the Airbound tail-sitter announcement, the Apollyon
field-test reel and the Aurus brand sizzle.

Structure: material macro -> machined hero reveal -> human hand -> field
deployment at dusk -> manufactured array -> typographic close. Monospace
technical annotations with leader lines sit over the plates, the way the
Airbound reference annotates its own hardware.

## Routing
Generated plates carry no legible glyph; every readable character is authored
in HyperFrames. Model choice per shot comes from the measured first loop and
from two new head-to-head tests written into this run: Veo against Kling on the
same product-hero prompt, and Veo against LTX on the same landscape prompt.

## Audio
No dialogue. Original locally synthesized industrial score and sound accents
from the bundled media-use library. No paid audio calls.

## Acceptance
Full MP4 decode; 1080p delivery; consistent object geometry across plates or an
explicit disclosure where it drifts; no overflow or overlapping text; contrast
passes on every annotation; agent visual review of every plate before selection.
