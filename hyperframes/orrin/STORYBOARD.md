# ORRIN / 30 seconds

Six hard cuts, no dissolves. The film is a manufacturing sequence: raw stock,
finished instrument, human hand, field, fleet, name. Motion is mechanical
throughout — see `design.md`.

## Frame 1
- status: outline
- src: compositions/material.html
- time: 0-4
- motion: rules `slow-push` on the plate to add movement the generated shot
  lacks, plus `type-on` for the first mono annotation.
- beat: `01 / MATERIAL` — carbon weave, annotated, nothing else.

## Frame 2
- status: outline
- src: compositions/instrument.html
- time: 4-10
- motion: `leader-line-draw` (authored: line draws to a terminal dot, then the
  label steps in) x3, staggered; `waterfall-entry` for the wordmark.
- beat: `02 / INSTRUMENT` — the object, three annotations, the name ORRIN.

## Frame 3
