# Visual direction
Midnight technical editorial. Ink #080f22; cobalt #3759ef; paper #edf3ff; muted ice #a8b9d5; amber #ffca77 for the concrete failure; mint #90edc4 for a captured/shared state. No serif typography, bakery imagery, talking heads, fake endorsements, or unsupported performance statistics.

1920x1080, 24fps. Safe margins 100px. Arial regular/bold stored locally, large 100–136px headlines and 28–38px UI. Precise 1px rules, tight typography, restrained corner radii. A custom angular trace-path mark and persistent product/category label introduce the product from frame zero.

One continuous screen surface owns the 9.5–27s workflow. Cursor actions have exact targets, press response, and settled states; report evidence enters in order. Camera scales from whole browser to useful detail; do not move text while it must be read. An isolated cool-blue hand insert motivates Record. UI/browser are authored, not generated glyphs.

Sound: new warm US female local voice; original sparse 108 BPM electronic score, quiet clicks at UI actions. Voice dominates. Final frame holds the CTA, never fades to black.
