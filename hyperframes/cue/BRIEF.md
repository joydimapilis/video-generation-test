# Cue: Make Room for the Idea

Create one original 30-second 16:9 startup launch concept, final H.264/AAC MP4.
User delegates decisions and wants only final videos for review. All new paid
experiments share a hard $10 estimated cap, including conservative reservations.

Cue is a fictional voice recorder and companion task app, not a real product
claim or customer testimonial. On-screen footer identifies the concept.
Target: small creative teams losing decisions between meetings and action.

## Direction
Human problem hook -> tactile product reveal -> one legible voice-to-task demo
-> short benefit -> branded close. Derived from six inspected library references.
All product footage and people are newly generated; no source faces, logos,
soundtracks, or marketing claims are reused. Deterministic HyperFrames for UI.

## Audio
Native generated hook dialogue, original locally synthesized electronic score,
small sound accents from the bundled media-use library. No paid music calls.

## Acceptance
Full MP4 decode; audible dialogue; consistent device; readable exact task text;
no overflow or overlapping text; 1080p delivery (720p model clips upscaled);
agent visual review and local speech transcript, no fabricated viewer testing.
