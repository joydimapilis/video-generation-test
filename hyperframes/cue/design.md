# Cue Design

Palette: charcoal #171a19, paper #f5f7f3, acid yellow #d8fa67,
forest #254d3b, coral #eb735d. No gradients, glow ornaments or background blobs.
The palette serves recording (coral), action (lime), calm focus (paper/charcoal).

Typography: local Arial bold for display and regular for UI. Tracking 0.
H1 140-180px; section statement 88-110px; UI 30-46px; labels minimum 26px.
Frame: 1920x1080, 96px safe margin. Actual product occupies full-bleed footage.
UI: deliberate close-up, one coherent workspace with rows, no nested cards.
Rounded corners at most 8px except actual circular controls/device anatomy.

Motion: one purposeful camera move per shot, state transitions tied to outcomes,
staggered task rows, short masked type arrivals. Avoid identical scene entrances.
Music: 112 BPM minimal marimba-like plucks, warm pad, restrained pulse.
