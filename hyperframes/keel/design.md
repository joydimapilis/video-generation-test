# KEEL design truth

Concept angle: the film argues by temperature. Cold and fast is the problem,
deep and dark is the turn, warm and slow is the answer - and the cut rate says
the same thing before the voice does.

## Palette
| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--ink` | `#05070a` | Black between acts, close card, caption scrim |
| `--paper` | `#f2efe9` | Captions, wordmark |
| `--muted` | `#8d8880` | Kicker, footer |
| `--brass` | `#c8a25c` | The single accent, act three and the close only |

No accent appears in acts one or two. The brass arrives with the warm act and is
the visual reward for staying through the dark middle.

## Type
- `KeelSerif` = Georgia / Georgia Bold, bundled. Wordmark and closing tagline
  only. A serif because the argument is about patience, and neither previous film
  used one.
- `KeelSans` = Arial / Arial Bold, bundled. Captions and the footer.

## Layout
- Captions: bottom centre, 44px, max width 1440px, one spoken line at a time, on
  a soft dark scrim so they stay legible over ten different plates.
- Footer: bottom right, small, holds for the whole runtime.
- No frame rule, no index, no HUD. ORRIN wore its chrome on the outside; this
  film keeps the frame clean so the footage carries it.

## Motion
- Act one: incoming clips whip in laterally over 0.16s with a short cyan flash on
  three of the cuts. Nothing settles.
- The turn: a full dip through black, 0.65s. The only moment the screen is empty.
- Acts two and three: opposing opacity envelopes, 0.4s, no movement on the cut.
- Close: the wordmark resolves, a brass rule draws, the tagline holds.

## Honesty
KEEL is fictional. No claim, statistic or product appears. The footer carries
`ORIGINAL CONCEPT / AI-GENERATED FILM` for the entire runtime.
